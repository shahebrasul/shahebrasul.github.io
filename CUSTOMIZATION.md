# Customization guide

## Quick edits
1. Open `assets/js/config.js` and update email, social URLs, CV path, and product count.
2. Open `content/portfolio-data.js` and replace sample entries with real work.
3. Edit the relevant page HTML for biography, role details, education, and experience.
4. Add your CV PDF to `assets/documents/`.
5. Add approved writing samples and story pages; link them from `writing.html`.

## Portfolio categories
- `technical`: Technical & SEO Content
- `website`: Website Copywriting
- `advertising`: Ad Copy & Advertising
- `scripts`: Scriptwriting
- `creative`: Creative Writing

Each item supports `title`, `category`, `categoryLabel`, `industry`, `audience`, `objective`, `intent`, `description`, `contribution`, `url`, and `placeholder`.

## Important
All initial portfolio records are placeholders. Replace them before presenting the website as a completed-work portfolio. Do not add client names, product details, metrics, credentials, or publication credits unless verified and approved for public use.
