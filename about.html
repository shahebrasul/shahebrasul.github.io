About / The person behind the work

A curious mind,
a considered practice.

I’m Shahebur Rasul — an engineer by education, a writer by profession, and a storyteller by instinct.

I’m interested in how people understand unfamiliar ideas, how information becomes useful, and how a well-shaped story can stay with someone.

“Understand first. Then find the clearest way to say it.”

My route into writing began with a technical education. Electrical and Electronic Engineering trained me to work through systems, examine how things function, and approach unfamiliar problems methodically. Those habits continue to shape how I research and write.

I now work as a Technical Content Writer in the software industry. The work involves moving between products, subjects, audiences, and search needs — learning enough about a topic to explain it accurately, then deciding what matters most to the reader.

Research before rhetoric

I prefer to understand the subject before deciding how to present it. That means gathering information, checking context, identifying the reader’s likely questions, and building a structure that makes the answer easy to follow. Good writing should feel natural; the thinking behind it should be deliberate.

More than one kind of writing

Professional assignments and fiction ask different things of a writer. One is shaped by a brief, an audience, and a practical objective. The other gives room to explore character, atmosphere, conflict, and possibility. I keep those practices distinct, while allowing each to strengthen the other.

Learning as part of the work

I believe a useful writing practice keeps evolving. I continue to develop my understanding of software, SEO, communication, and the tools that support research and production. This site will grow alongside that work.
